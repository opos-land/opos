OPOS programme module: wireframes

Screens of the programme module for four conventions (Nordcon, Fantasmagoria, Dni Fantastyki, Pyrkon) and a Worldcon stress test. Wireframes for discussion, not designs: sample data only, convention settings are assumptions, and all people shown are invented. See the brief beside this folder for the model, the feature matrix and the open questions.

  • 14. Panel: the programme during the convention (4 screens)
