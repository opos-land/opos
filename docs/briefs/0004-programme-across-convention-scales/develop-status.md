# Programme across convention scales: what develop has

- Date: 2026-11-23
- Status: non-binding ([briefs](../README.md)); a companion of
  [brief 0004](../0004-programme-across-convention-scales.md)
- Compared: the running application, `develop` as of 2026-11-23, against the brief's model
  ([4](../0004-programme-across-convention-scales.md#4-the-model)), its feature matrix
  ([5](../0004-programme-across-convention-scales.md#5-the-feature-matrix)), its screens and role
  journeys ([6](../0004-programme-across-convention-scales.md#6-screens-index)), section
  [7](../0004-programme-across-convention-scales.md#7-what-changes-against-docsproductmd) and
  [PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md)
- How it was checked: the local stack with the seed's demo data; each role the seed holds signed
  in to the portal, the panel and the admin application and clicked through in a browser, at a
  desktop and a phone width, about 220 screens in all. Nothing was saved, liked or deleted, so
  what a write does is read from the screens and the specs, not tried. The screenshots stay
  outside the repository.

Status words: **done** (a screen and a route do it), **partly** (what is missing is named),
**missing** (no screen, route or API), **contradicts** (the application does something the brief
rules out). Routes write `:c` for a convention's address and `:e` for an edition's. Every role and
capability below is the brief's; none is a rule of the application.

## Summary

The programme module is still the minimum of
[PRODUCT.md 5.1](../../PRODUCT.md#51-permissions-and-roles): an item is a title, a description, a
start and an end, with up to ten speakers who must be confirmed accounts, added by email. The panel
lists, creates, edits and deletes items; the portal lists them by day, shows an item with its
speakers' public names linked to their profiles, and lets a signed-in account like an item and see
its liked items in My schedule. Nothing of the brief's model exists yet: no dictionaries, rooms or
blocks, no draft or publication of items, no programme person, cast roles or confirmation, no
switches, sign-ups, call for proposals, changelog, notifications, import, export or Programme
area. Around the programme, develop built much that the brief treated as absent or future, all of
it after the brief's date: file uploads and images, the account's public profile, the tickets
shop with its scanner, and the home page's edition list with an edition page. Those change what
the programme's first spec builds on: a speaker's public face, a photo, the ticket type that
entitlements must extend, the scanner a room mode would join, and the page that says when the
programme comes out.

## Other findings

- **The home page states the target.** Its copy offers attendees to sign up for an edition and
  review what they attended, and calls OPOS the platform for "programme, tickets and attendee
  sign-ups"; neither attendee registrations nor reviews are built. PRODUCT.md 11 keeps both in the
  MVP, but a visitor reads the copy as what exists.
- **Spec statuses.** The tickets specs 0002–0011 and the specs of file storage and image variants
  say `Draft` while their code is merged and running; whether they stay open or wait for their
  status is for the team to say ([specs](../../specs/README.md)).
- **No programme on the edition's dashboard.** A programme org's dashboard shows the edition's
  state alone; the brief's today's dashboard and programme status have no place yet.
- **Programme read sees a form.** An item opens for programme read as the write form, disabled,
  with a greyed Save, where the brief draws read-only screens as text
  ([ab-role-settings-readonly](wireframes/j09-org-with-programme-read-seeing-without-acting.html#ab-role-settings-readonly)).
